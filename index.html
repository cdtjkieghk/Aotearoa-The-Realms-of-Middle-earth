    A note from the mapmaker

    This is a fan-made, Middle-earth-inspired map of real places in Aotearoa New Zealand. The fantasy names are playful references; the pins mark actual towns, landscapes and well-known filming locations.

    The basemap is Esri World Topographic Map, with Esri attribution retained in the map corner. Switch to Esri World Imagery to see the landscape beneath the parchment tint.

    Distance measurements are straight-line distances, not driving directions. The Fellowship trail is an illustrative sequence, not a navigable route. Please check access, weather and local conservation guidance before visiting remote places.
